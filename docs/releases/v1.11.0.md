# v1.11.0 — readable colours, and Allowance is back

Three pairs of colours in the app were below the contrast everything else holds
itself to, and all three were found by measuring rather than by looking.

The pie chart's legend drew each category's icon in that category's colour on a
14% tint of the same colour — 1.74:1 for Allowance, 1.91:1 for Food. The icon was
the one thing in the row you could not see. It is now a darker shade of the same
hue, which clears 5:1 for every category and still reads as a colour key.

The amount figures in light mode were 3.41:1 (income) and 4.38:1 (expense) on a
white row, under WCAG AA for the size of text they colour. They are darker now,
with the hue unchanged: green still means money in, red money out. The same
correction reaches the PDF report, where the figures were 3.15:1 on their panel.

Fuel, Allowance and Coffee Shop had no icon of their own on one screen or the
other, so a row could be a generic box while the pie chart knew its colour.

**Allowance is back.** v1.10.0 renamed it to Pocket Money and v1.10.1 stopped
offering it for new transactions; both are undone. It is a normal income
subcategory again, under the name it had before. The upgrade renames the existing
row in place, so a transaction you already recorded under Pocket Money keeps
pointing at the same subcategory and only the label changes — nothing is
duplicated and nothing is lost.

**Restoring an older backup.** A snapshot taken while Pocket Money existed names
it in the file. Restoring one, by file or by Drive sync, now puts those
transactions under Allowance rather than creating a second subcategory beside it.
If you had made a Pocket Money of your own, that one is left alone.

**Two smaller things.** The Review button on the update notice now opens Settings
with the Settings tab highlighted, instead of showing the page while the bar still
said Home. And the old summary card, which the redesign had left unreferenced, is
gone.

**Upgrading.** Your data is untouched; the app upgrades its database on first
launch, which takes no noticeable time. Your categories, transactions and Google
Drive sign-in are kept.

**Installing.** Download the APK attached to this release, or check for updates
in Settings. An older debug-signed build must be uninstalled before installing
the release-signed APK; uninstalling deletes local data.

**Google Drive access.** The OAuth consent screen is in testing; only approved
test accounts can use Drive backup. The app requests the `drive.file` scope and
uploads only when you start a backup.

**Privacy.** The app has no analytics, ads, or application server. The update
check contacts GitHub's public release API. Android Auto Backup and
device-to-device transfer are disabled, so keep a separate backup of your data.
